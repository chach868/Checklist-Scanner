# Checklist Scanner

Turn a photo of a paper or whiteboard task list into a checklist on your phone. Check items off while you work. Checked items stay on the list, so when you're back at the master list you can see what to mark.

**Open it:** https://chach868.github.io/Checklist-Scanner/

## How to use it
1. Tap **New checklist** and take a photo of the list, or choose a photo, screenshot, PDF, or .txt/.csv file. You can also type or paste the list.
2. Check the list it read, fix anything that came out wrong, and save. Items already ticked on the paper come in checked.
3. Tap items to check them off as you go. Each item keeps its number from the master list.
4. Back at the master list, mark off what's checked. Use **⋯ → Uncheck everything** to reuse the list.

Tip: on your phone, use your browser's **Add to Home screen** so it opens like an app.

## Privacy
Everything runs on your phone. Photos and lists are never uploaded; lists are saved in your browser on that phone.

## Reading photos
Text recognition runs on the phone (Tesseract.js). It reads printed lists, work orders, and screenshots well, and handwriting or whiteboards poorly. The first photo needs internet to download the text reader (about 10 MB).

Have a Claude account? The [Claude version](https://claude.ai/artifact/5sv7hmmSoUU18mh78sgnSG) reads handwriting and whiteboards much better. Open it in Chrome or Safari, signed in to claude.ai.
